# 12 — Seed: Starter Shopping List

Starter content for the "Daftar Belanja Bangunan". The PRD's original list came from AI suggestions and was never verified, and this one is **also a suggestion**, written for a small duck-meat cold room at −18 °C to −20 °C. Ask a cold-storage contractor to review it. Everything is editable by an admin after launch (R-SH1). Approved by the owner as starter content (Nov 2026).

Only No. 5 (Breather Valve, text from the PRD) and section 4 ("Panel Listrik & Pengaman", from PRD UC03) come from the PRD. The rest are proposals, built around the small items the PRD says usually get forgotten.
